Policies

Good to know before booking

Insurance & qualification

WSDS holds a UK CAA Flyer ID and Operator ID and is qualified to fly commercially.

Weather

Free reschedule if conditions aren't safe to fly — no cancellation fee. If conditions turn mid-shoot and WSDS has to leave and return another day, no return call-out fee is charged. Each drone's wind resistance is explained during the free Zoom consultation. Wind speed on site is measured with a handheld wind meter, and if it exceeds those limits during a session, WSDS must stop flying unless conditions are expected to calm down later that day.

Licensing

WSDS keeps the copyright in all footage. Once you have paid in full, you can use your footage for your own marketing or personal use, with no time limit. The full wording is in the terms and conditions.

Rescheduling

Free of charge. If WSDS has already arrived on site for the shoot when you reschedule, the £10 return call-out fee applies, on one-off jobs and subscriptions alike.

Cancelling

Free of charge before the shoot. If you cancel while WSDS is on site, the £10 return call-out fee applies. If you cancel after the shoot, you are charged half the package price.

Travel

Included for shoots in West Sussex. Further afield, travel is charged at 20p per mile for the miles beyond the West Sussex border, there and back.

Return call-outs

£10 fee if a one-off job requires WSDS to return to site. Subscription clients are not charged, other than for an on-site reschedule.

Site hazards

WSDS will ask about power lines, overhead cables and any other potential hazards near the property or flying area during the Zoom consultation, so the flight can be planned around them safely.

1Subscriptions

  1. 1.1Subscriptions are billed monthly. Starter includes 3 shoots a month, Growth includes 5, and Partner has a 7-shoot monthly minimum with each further shoot at the same per-shoot rate.

2Payment, minimum term and cancelling

Applies to every subscription: Starter, Growth and Partner.

  1. 2.1Payment in advance. Each month is paid before any of that month's shoots take place. Your payment reserves our days and your shoots for that month.
  2. 2.2Minimum term. Every subscription runs for at least 3 months.
  3. 2.3No notice period. After the minimum term you can stop at any time before your next payment is due. Your subscription then ends at the end of the month you have already paid for.

  5. 2.5Your payment date does not move. After a late payment, the next one is still due on the original day of the following month.
  6. 2.6After 7 days. If a payment has not been received within 7 days of the date it was due, the footage we are holding is deleted.

3Unused shoots

Applies to every subscription: Starter, Growth and Partner.
