Why aerial footage

The one shot that makes people stop scrolling.

A ground-level photo shows a room, a stall, a doorway. An aerial shot shows the whole picture — the setting, the scale, the context that's usually missing from everyday marketing.

It changes how people relate to a space before they've set foot in it. Instead of imagining a location from a handful of close-up photos, they see it properly: how it sits in its surroundings, how big it really is, what it feels like to arrive.

That's what Drone Visuals adds to a shoot. Not just another photo for the gallery, but the one shot that makes people stop scrolling, understand a space at a glance, and picture themselves there. Everything else — how often you shoot, what you do with the footage — is just how much you want that working for you.

Policies

Good to know before booking.

Insurance & qualification

Drone Visuals holds a UK CAA Flyer ID and Operator ID and is qualified to fly commercially.

Weather

Free reschedule if conditions aren't safe to fly — no cancellation fee. If conditions turn mid-shoot and Drone Visuals has to leave and return another day, no return call-out fee is charged. Each drone's wind resistance is explained during the free Zoom consultation. Wind speed on site is measured with a handheld wind meter, and if it exceeds those limits during a session, Drone Visuals must stop flying unless conditions are expected to calm down later that day.

Licensing

Drone Visuals keeps the copyright in all footage. Once you have paid in full, you can use your footage for your own marketing or personal use, with no time limit. The full wording is in the terms and conditions.

Rescheduling

Free of charge. If Drone Visuals has already arrived on site for the shoot when you reschedule, the £10 return call-out fee applies, on one-off jobs and subscriptions alike.

Cancelling

Free of charge before the shoot. If you cancel while Drone Visuals is on site, the £10 return call-out fee applies. If you cancel after the shoot, you are charged half the package price.

Add-ons

Add-ons are only available alongside a package or subscription shoot — they can't be booked on their own.

Travel

Included for shoots in West Sussex. Further afield, travel is charged at 20p per mile for the miles beyond the West Sussex border, there and back.

Return call-outs

£10 fee if a one-off job requires Drone Visuals to return to site. Subscription clients are not charged, other than for an on-site reschedule.

Site hazards

Drone Visuals will ask about power lines, overhead cables and any other potential hazards near the property or flying area during the Zoom consultation, so the flight can be planned around them safely.
